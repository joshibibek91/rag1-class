# Himalayan Courtyard Guesthouse — Staff and Guest Handbook

Thamel, Kathmandu. This handbook is a fictional example, written for
practising document search. It is not a real business.

## 1. About the Guesthouse

Himalayan Courtyard is a family-run guesthouse in a quiet lane of Thamel,
five minutes on foot from the Garden of Dreams. It has twelve rooms around
a brick courtyard with a small garden. The family has welcomed travellers
since 2009, many of them trekkers on their way to or from the Annapurna and
Everest regions.

The guesthouse aims to be a calm base in a busy part of the city. Quiet
hours are from 10 p.m. to 7 a.m. Staff live on site, and someone is always
at the front desk, day and night.

## 2. Rooms and Rates

All prices are per room, per night, in Nepali rupees, and include breakfast
for the number of guests shown. Prices are higher from October to November
and from March to April, the main trekking seasons.

| Single         | 1      | 2,200            | 3,000             |

Children under six stay free when sharing a bed with their parents. An extra
mattress costs NPR 600 per night. Long stays of seven nights or more receive
ten percent off the total, applied at check-out.

## 3. Check-in Procedure for Front Desk Staff

Follow these steps for every arriving guest, in this order. Do not skip a
step, even for returning guests.

1. Greet the guest and offer a glass of water or tea.
2. Find the reservation in the booking system under the guest's family name.
3. Ask to see a passport, or a citizenship card for Nepali guests.
4. Photograph the identity document and attach it to the reservation.
5. Confirm the number of nights and the room type with the guest.
6. Take the deposit for any unpaid amount, as described in section 5.
7. Give the guest a room key and a printed wifi card.
8. Explain breakfast times and quiet hours.
9. Mark the reservation as "checked in" in the booking system.

Check-in starts at 1 p.m. Guests arriving earlier may leave luggage in the
store room behind the front desk, free of charge.

## 4. Reservation Withdrawal

A guest who no longer wishes to stay may withdraw a reservation by email or
in person at the front desk. Withdrawals made more than 48 hours before the
arrival date receive a full refund of any deposit. Withdrawals made within
48 hours of arrival forfeit the first night's charge, and the rest of the
deposit is refunded.

During peak season, the notice period is seven days instead of 48 hours.
Guests who simply do not arrive, without any notice, are charged for the
first night, and the rest of the reservation is released.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method within ten working
days. Refunds for cash deposits are given in rupees at the front desk.

## 5. Payments and Deposits

The guesthouse accepts cash in Nepali rupees, cards (Visa and Mastercard),
and the eSewa and Khalti digital wallets. Card payments carry a three
percent fee, charged by the bank. Foreign currency is not accepted at the
desk; a money exchange is two minutes away on the main road.

A deposit of one night's rate is taken at the time of reservation. The
balance is paid at check-in. Receipts are printed on request, and an
electronic receipt is always sent by email.

## 6. Breakfast and Meals

Breakfast is served in the courtyard from 7 to 10 a.m. It includes tea or
coffee, eggs cooked to order, toast, seasonal fruit and Tibetan bread.
Vegetarian and vegan options are always available; please tell the kitchen
about any allergy the evening before.

Packed breakfasts can be prepared for guests leaving before 7 a.m. Ask at
the front desk by 8 p.m. the day before. Lunch and dinner are not served,
but staff are happy to recommend nearby restaurants.

## 7. Wifi and Power

Free wifi covers every room and the courtyard. The network name and password
are printed on the card given at check-in, and change every month. The
guesthouse has a backup battery system, so the wifi and the room lights stay
on during power cuts. Hot water comes from solar heaters, with an electric
backup on cloudy days.

## 8. The Trekking Desk

The trekking desk helps guests plan treks and tours. Staff can arrange
licensed guides and porters, TIMS cards and national park permits, and
transport to Pokhara or Lukla. Booking a guide requires two days' notice in
peak season. The desk is open from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.

Guests on a trek may store luggage at the guesthouse for up to 30 days at
no charge, as long as they have a reservation for their return.

## 9. Airport Transfers

A private car to or from Tribhuvan International Airport costs NPR 1,200
during the day and NPR 1,500 between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m. Drivers wait in the
arrivals hall holding a sign with the guest's name. Please share the flight
number when making the reservation.

## 10. Lost Property

Items left behind are kept for three months. Staff record each item in the
lost property book, with the date and the room number. To ask about an item,
email the front desk with a description and the dates of the stay. Items
can be posted abroad if the guest pays the postage.

## 11. Health and Safety

Each floor has a fire extinguisher and a printed evacuation map. The
assembly point is the open square at the end of the lane. A first-aid kit
is kept at the front desk, and staff can call a doctor at any hour. Drinking
water from the taps is not safe; filtered water is free on every floor.

## 12. Behaviour and Respect

Guests are asked to respect quiet hours and the family's privacy. Smoking
is allowed only in the corner of the courtyard marked for it. Guests may
invite visitors to the courtyard until 9 p.m. The guesthouse may ask a guest
to leave, without a refund, for violence or serious damage to property.
